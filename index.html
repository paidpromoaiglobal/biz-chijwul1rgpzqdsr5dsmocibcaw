Our Story

Made with Passion

Selvamani Canteen, nestled in the heart of Town Hall, Coimbatore, is a beloved local eatery that has earned the trust and love of thousands of food enthusiasts across the city. Located at 18, Ismail Street, our canteen is a go-to destination for anyone craving authentic, home-style South Indian meals prepared fresh every day. With a stellar rating of 4.1 out of 5 from over 1,500 happy patrons, we take immense pride in the quality and consistency of every dish we serve.

Open seven days a week from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM, Selvamani Canteen caters to early risers, busy lunch crowds, and late-evening diners alike. Whether you are stopping by for a hearty breakfast, a filling lunch thali, or a satisfying dinner, our kitchen is always ready to welcome you. We believe great food is the foundation of great memories, and every visit to Selvamani Canteen is crafted to feel just like home.
